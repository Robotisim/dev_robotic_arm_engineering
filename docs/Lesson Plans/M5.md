

# Module 5 Lesson Plan

## M5_V01_Why_Pick_and_Place_Is_the_First_Manipulation_Task_[F]

### Purpose

Introduce pick/place as the first complete manipulation workflow.

### Story question

> We can now plan safe motions, but how do we turn those motions into a useful robot task?

### Content

* Define pick and place.
* Explain why it is more than one movement.
* Explain the full sequence.
* Show what success means.
* Explain that targets are fixed in this module.
* Explain that perception comes later.

### Output

Students understand the purpose and boundaries of Module 5.

## M5_V02_Task_Decomposition_Approach_Grasp_Lift_Place_Retreat_[A]

### Purpose

Teach the pick/place sequence visually.

### Story question

> Why can’t the robot just move directly to the object and close the gripper?

### Content

Explain:

```text
home
→ pre-grasp
→ approach
→ grasp
→ attach/close gripper
→ lift
→ move to place
→ release
→ retreat
→ home
```

Explain why each stage exists:

* pre-grasp aligns the tool
* approach reduces contact uncertainty
* grasp closes at the right pose
* lift creates clearance
* place moves safely to target
* retreat avoids collision after release

Your Module 5 plan emphasizes approach/grasp/lift/place/retreat as the core task decomposition.

### Output

Students understand manipulation as a sequence, not a single command.
